Write to the keepers

Mail support@aethergems.link. Tell us the phone model, the Android version, and what the court was doing when something went wrong. A reply usually leaves within two working days.

Things players ask

Do crystals turn into cash?
No. Crystals, rare stones and patron gifts exist only inside the arenas. There is no shop, no wager and no payout.
The save disappeared.
Progress sits on the device itself. A reinstall, a cleared storage, or a different phone starts the climb from the first cloud court again. We cannot restore a ledger that never left the handset.
The court will not open.
Check that the phone has a working connection, then try the arena again. If the loading screen stays up, force-close Aether Gems and reopen it. If that still fails, write to us with the model name.
Sound or vibration will not quiet down.
Both switches live in the in-game settings plate. The system volume still governs how loud the phone can be.
Where is the privacy notice?
It is the privacy notice for this edition.
