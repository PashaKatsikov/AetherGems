Privacy notice

Aether Gems · marble courts above the clouds · 23 September 2026

Aether Gems is a chain-lightning pastime on floating Greek courts. Crystals you gather are game tokens inside the arena. They are not money, they cannot be withdrawn, and the game does not take wagers or sell goods.

This notice describes the small set of details the game handles. It is written for the Android edition published as Aether Gems.

What stays on the handset

Court progress, crystal totals, upgrade ranks, unlocked patrons, sound and vibration choices, and whether the opening lesson has been seen are written into ordinary app storage on the device. We do not upload that ledger to a server of ours. Clearing the app’s storage, or removing the app, drops it.

Install measurement

To see which introduction brought a new player, the game uses AppsFlyer. That service may receive an advertising identifier supplied by the operating system and an install identifier that AppsFlyer assigns. Those values are used to count installs and to judge whether a campaign reached anyone. They are not a profile of how you play a particular court.

Plain technical notes

While the game runs it can see the device model, the Android release, the language you chose for the system, and whether a network path is up. That information keeps the arena drawing correctly and tells a failed connection apart from a finished match. We do not read your contacts, photos, microphone, precise map position, or payment instruments. The game does not ask you to create an account.

The only detail that leaves the handset for a vendor is the install measurement described above.

Who receives those notes

The advertising identifier and the install identifier go to AppsFlyer, which measures attribution for us. We do not sell personal information, and we do not pass a player list to data brokers. If the law requires a disclosure, we would answer the specific request rather than a standing share.

How long the notes last

AppsFlyer keeps attribution records under its own schedule and our instructions to that vendor: long enough to credit an install, then deleted or folded into statistics that no longer point at a device. Anything saved only on the phone lasts until you erase it.

Choices you already have

Android lets you reset or limit the advertising identifier. Doing so does not delete crystals already stored on the device. Uninstalling Aether Gems removes the local save.

Younger players

Aether Gems is drawn for a general audience of adults and older teens. It is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and questions

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will move with it. Write to support@aethergems.link. Practical help with a stuck court is on the support page.
